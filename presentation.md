# **CityPulse**

**Your AI-Powered Urban Adventure Planner**

---

## **Core Concept**

CityPulse is a smart travel assistant that helps users create personalized itineraries for exploring cities. It combines AI-powered recommendations with real-time information to create a seamless and engaging travel experience.

---

## **Key Features**

- **AI-Powered Itinerary Planner**
- **Interactive Map View**
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**
- **Real-time Push Notifications**
- **Explore & Discover**
- **User Profile & Settings**

---

## **AI-Powered Itinerary Planner**

- **Chat-based Interface:** Plan your trip by chatting with our AI assistant.
- **Personalized Suggestions:** Get recommendations for points of interest (POIs), restaurants, and events based on your preferences ("vibes").
- **Dynamic Itinerary Generation:** The app generates a complete itinerary with a timeline.
- **Save & Manage:** Users can save and manage multiple itineraries.

---

## **Interactive Map View**

- **Visualize Your Itinerary:** See your entire trip plotted on an interactive map.
- **Custom Directions:** Get directions between points of interest.
- **User Location:** The map can center on your current location to help with navigation.

---

## **Progressive Web App (PWA)**

- **Installable:** Install CityPulse on your phone or desktop like a native app.
- **Offline Access:** Access your itineraries even without an internet connection.
- **App-like Experience:** Enjoy a fast, reliable, and engaging full-screen experience.

---

## **Real-time Push Notifications**

- **Stay Updated:** Receive timely alerts about your itinerary.
- **Customizable Alerts:** Users can choose what types of notifications they want to receive (e.g., traffic, events).
- **Opt-in Control:** Full control over enabling or disabling notifications.

---

## **Explore & Discover**

- **Curated Content:** Discover new places and activities through our explore grid.
- **Vibe-based Filtering:** Find things to do that match your current mood or "vibe".
- **Rich Content Cards:** View details about places with images and descriptions.

---

## **User Profile & Settings**

- **Personalized Space:** A dedicated profile section for each user.
- **Notification Management:** Easily manage push notification settings.
- **Goal Tracking:** Set and track travel goals.

---
